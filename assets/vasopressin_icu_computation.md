# Vasopressin ICU Computation & Dosing Reference Guide

Vasopressin (Argipressin / Antidiuretic Hormone) is a potent non-adrenergic vasopressor used in critical care settings, primarily as a second-line agent for vasodilatory and septic shock. Unlike catecholamines (e.g., norepinephrine, epinephrine), vasopressin is **not weight-based** and is administered as a fixed continuous infusion measured in **units per minute (units/min)**.

---

## 1. Master Formula for IV Pump Infusion Rate

To calculate the hourly flow rate in **milliliters per hour (mL/hr)** for a smart IV pump:

$$\text{Flow Rate (mL/hr)} = \frac{\text{Prescribed Dose (units/min)} \times 60 \text{ min/hr}}{\text{Concentration (units/mL)}}$$

---

## 2. Common ICU Preparations & Calculations

### Standard Concentration: 20 units in 100 mL (0.2 units/mL)
> **Concentration Calculation:** $\frac{20 \text{ units}}{100 \text{ mL}} = \mathbf{0.2 \text{ units/mL}}$

#### Scenario A: Standard Fixed Septic Shock Dose ($0.03 \text{ units/min}$)
1. **Convert to hourly dose:**
   $$0.03 \text{ units/min} \times 60 \text{ min/hr} = \mathbf{1.8 \text{ units/hr}}$$
2. **Calculate IV Pump Rate:**
   $$\text{Rate (mL/hr)} = \frac{1.8 \text{ units/hr}}{0.2 \text{ units/mL}} = \mathbf{9.0 \text{ mL/hr}}$$

#### Scenario B: Initial Titration Starting Dose ($0.01 \text{ units/min}$)
1. **Convert to hourly dose:**
   $$0.01 \text{ units/min} \times 60 \text{ min/hr} = \mathbf{0.6 \text{ units/hr}}$$
2. **Calculate IV Pump Rate:**
   $$\text{Rate (mL/hr)} = \frac{0.6 \text{ units/hr}}{0.2 \text{ units/mL}} = \mathbf{3.0 \text{ mL/hr}}$$

---

### High-Concentration (Fluid-Restricted): 40 units in 100 mL (0.4 units/mL)
> **Concentration Calculation:** $\frac{40 \text{ units}}{100 \text{ mL}} = \mathbf{0.4 \text{ units/mL}}$

* **Target Dose:** $0.03 \text{ units/min}$ ($1.8 \text{ units/hr}$)
* **Calculation:**
  $$\text{Rate (mL/hr)} = \frac{1.8 \text{ units/hr}}{0.4 \text{ units/mL}} = \mathbf{4.5 \text{ mL/hr}}$$

## 4. Key Clinical & Safety Reminders

* **Line Administration:** Vasopressin is a potent vesicant. Always administer via a **central venous line** whenever possible to prevent tissue ischemia and extravasation necrosis.
* **Non-Titrated Fixed Dosing:** Unlike norepinephrine, vasopressin is usually maintained at a steady rate ($0.03 \text{ units/min}$) rather than continually titrated up and down based on blood pressure fluctuations.
* **Monitoring Parameters:**
  * **Hemodynamics:** MAP, SVR, heart rate.
  * **Perfusional/Ischemic Checks:** Digits (fingers/toes), skin temperature, urinary output, cardiac rhythm, and abdominal assessment (mesenteric ischemia risk).
  * **Electrolytes:** Serum sodium levels (risk of hyponatremia due to V2 receptor stimulation/water retention).
