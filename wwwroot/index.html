📝 Drug Calculations Made Easy

Quick Drug Calculation Formulas with Worked Examples

💊 1. Tablet Calculation

Required Quantity = Prescribed Dose ÷ Available Dose
Worked Example:
• Prescribed Dose = 500 mg
• Available Dose = 250 mg / tablet
• Answer: 500 ÷ 250 = 2 Tablets

🧪 2. Liquid Calculation

Required Vol = (Prescribed ÷ Available) × Vol Available
Worked Example:
• Prescribed Dose = 250 mg
• Available Dose = 125 mg / 5 mL
• Answer: (250 ÷ 125) × 5 = 10 mL

💉 3. Injection Calculation

Required Vol = (Prescribed ÷ Available) × Diluent Vol
Worked Example:
• Prescribed Dose = 500 mg
• Available Dose = 1 g (1000 mg) / 10 mL
• Answer: (500 ÷ 1000) × 10 = 5 mL

💧 4. IV Flow Rate (mL/hr)

Flow Rate (mL/hr) = Total Volume (mL) ÷ Time (hours)
Worked Example:
• Total Volume = 1000 mL over 8 hours
• Answer: 1000 ÷ 8 = 125 mL/hr

⏱️ 5. Drops Per Minute (gtt/min)

Flow Rate (gtt/min) = (Volume × Drop Factor) ÷ Time (min)
Worked Example:
• 500 mL over 4 hours (240 min), Drop Factor = 20 gtt/mL
• Answer: (500 × 20) ÷ 240 = 42 gtt/min

⚖️ 6. Weight-Based Dosing

Required Dose = Dose/kg × Patient Weight (kg)
Worked Example:
• Dose/kg = 15 mg/kg, Patient Weight = 20 kg
• Answer: 15 × 20 = 300 mg
